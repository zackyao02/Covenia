# Covenia redesign direction · service evidence dossier

Brief: A customer-service operator inside a Qianniu-style workbench must see the existing evidence, who owes the next step, and the next safe action within seconds. Covenia is a service-continuity instrument, not a consumer landing page.

## Tokens

- Host navy `#25466B`: remains reserved for the Qianniu-style workbench.
- Porcelain `#F6F8F7`: Covenia canvas, drawn from a beauty product quality desk rather than warm lifestyle editorial.
- Graphite `#23353B`: primary type and service record.
- Ink plum `#503447`: Covenia's generated mark and the one primary action surface.
- Frost `#E8ECEA`: evidence and reference surfaces.
- Signal coral `#B75D50`: exception and overdue cues only.
- Typography: `Microsoft YaHei UI`/`PingFang SC` for Chinese operations, `Aptos Display`/Arial for the Latin wordmark. 26px action title, 19px consumer, 14px body, 12px metadata. Avoid decorative serif.

## Layout

```text
Qianniu host: [sessions] [conversation] | Covenia dossier
                                      | wordmark + mode
                                      | current consumer / queue
                                      | one strong next-action panel
                                      | evidence contact sheet + status
                                      | responsibility / next update
                                      | expandable detailed record
```

Left alignment follows operational reading order. The contact sheet uses the case's actual demo evidence assets, not decorative stock imagery. A single dark action panel is the memorable element; all other sections stay quiet.

## Rejection of generic defaults

The prior warm cream + plum + copper/serif treatment could apply to any luxury dashboard. Three pale cards and the oversized `03` used decoration to fill space. Replace those with a cooler document-like surface, case-specific proof strip, clear status text, functional separators and one primary action. Keep plum only where the Covenia mark or an action needs identity. The queue number remains because it is a real order; arbitrary action numbering goes away. Motion should explain a state transition, not decorate every tile.
