# Covenia visual system · 2 October 2026

Subject: a service-continuity copilot inside a practical Qianniu-like workbench. The operator must recognize the next action, the proof behind it, and who owns the follow-up without decoding an AI dashboard.

## Direction

- **Palette:** abyss `#0B1E2A`, deep teal `#174B53`, glacial cyan `#66E0D1`, electric coral `#FF6A62`, frost `#F5FBFC`, ink `#17363E`. Status tints stay localized, never filling every card.
- **Type:** `Noto Sans SC` for Chinese and `Segoe UI Variable` for Latin/figures, with system fallbacks. Headlines use 600 weight and natural Chinese spacing; body never drops below 12px in the extension's primary views.
- **Surface:** translucent floating glass cards with 18px soft radii, directional shadows, inner highlight and restrained blur; 11px radii for fields and buttons. The background supplies depth with soft teal and coral light sources. Avoid stacking equally bright cards.
- **Layout:** host remains operational; plugin reads top to bottom as case → next action → evidence → obligation. In progress state: current obligation → receipt → milestones. Confirmation is a focused paper sheet with one strong commit button.
- **Brand mark:** a sculptural glass relay knot representing a continuous handoff. Generated as a transparent bitmap for the 3D version, with a native SVG fallback kept in the asset folder.

The previous plum action card and pastel progress cards created separate visual dialects. This pass follows the user's explicit cyber-luxury direction: high spatial depth and small high-saturation light accents around a clear service workflow. Glass affects the shell, not the legibility of text or the meaning of status.
